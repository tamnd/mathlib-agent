## Task

The declarations below have depth E1, no family, and the same name shape. Propose E0 families for them (spec 09.4). A family is a pattern and one sentence that tells the reader what each member says, so that no member needs its own E1 sentence.

1. Give the pattern in the form of `blueprint/families.yaml`: name, kind, attr, typeHead, typeHas, twin, deprecated. Use the fewest fields that select the members and no other declaration.
2. Give the text in plain words. Use `{op}` only when the family has ops.
3. Say which members of the group the family leaves out, and why.
4. Give the family the depth E0 and an id of the form F-<words>.

Add the families to `blueprint/families.yaml` in the branch of the run. A person accepts each family.
