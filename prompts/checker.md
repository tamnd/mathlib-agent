## Task

Check the section below against the Lean types of its declarations at the commit of the run. You get the Lean types, not the view of the writer.

Give a list of faults. For each fault give the place in the section, the kind and a short reason. The kinds are:

- a claim that does not match a Lean type;
- a missing hypothesis;
- a proof gap;
- a wrong reference;
- a style fault against spec 07.

Do not change the section. A person reads the list and the writer fixes the faults. When you find no fault, say so in one line.
