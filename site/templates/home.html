The Mathlib Handbook

A textbook of all the mathematics in Mathlib

The handbook explains each declaration of Mathlib at a depth that fits its role. The main definitions and theorems get a full treatment with proofs and examples. The other declarations get a short proof, one sentence or a place in a family. The text follows Mathlib master, and each stable Mathlib tag gives an edition in PDF and EPUB.
